Delete your account

How to remove your Redemzon online account and what happens to your information.

Effective date: 10 November 2026 · Contact: thinkingcatstudios@gmail.com

Important: Web deletion removes your cloud account immediately. An installed Redemzon app clears that account's local database and avatar files the next time it opens, resumes, or reconnects to the internet. Until then, its local copy remains on the device. The web page cannot remove exports or provider backup copies.

Option 1: Delete in the app

  1. Open Redemzon and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Settings → Storage.
  3. Choose Delete account and review the confirmation prompt.
  4. Confirm Delete account.

The app first removes files under your account in its private avatar storage bucket. If that deletion fails, it stops and reports an error rather than continuing. If it succeeds, the app calls the account-deletion function; the authentication account and rows linked to it are deleted, then the app clears its local database and avatar files, signs out, and returns to the start screen.

Delete your online account on this page

Enter the registered email address. We will send a one-time code to verify account access. After verification, you will review a final confirmation before deletion.

Delete your account? This permanently deletes your cloud account, synced records, and cloud avatars. This cannot be undone. Any installed Redemzon app will erase this account's local data when it next connects to the internet; until then, that local copy remains on the device.

Delete from the app instead

In the app, use Settings → Storage → Delete account. The in-app process also clears the app's local database and avatar files after cloud deletion succeeds. If you submit a request by email instead, write from your registered address to thinkingcatstudios@gmail.com. Do not send your password or sign-in code. Email requests are handled manually after verification.

What is deleted

  • Your Supabase authentication account.
  • Cloud database rows linked by cascading account ownership, including synced notes, diary entries, profile details, Alter Ego profiles, reward progress, and reward-claim records present in the current schema.
  • Objects under your account folder in the private alter-ego-avatars storage bucket, when the deletion request completes successfully.

What is not deleted by this process

  • Copies you exported, shared, or otherwise saved outside the app.
  • Provider backup copies, which may remain for a period controlled by the provider. The app code does not define or verify a backup purge schedule.

Timeline

For an in-app request, deletion runs when you confirm. The app reports success only after cloud account deletion and local database/avatar cleanup both succeed. If cloud removal succeeds but device cleanup fails, the app reports that the cloud account is deleted and local cleanup needs attention. For this web form, cloud deletion starts after email verification and final confirmation, then removes avatar files and deletes the account. A minimal account-deletion notice lets an installed app detect the deletion and clear its local data the next time it opens, resumes, or reconnects. Until that online check succeeds, the local copy remains on the device. Email requests are processed after verification, but the product currently publishes no fixed maximum completion time. We cannot promise an exact backup-erasure date from the available provider configuration.
